01 — Overview

Why Platter feels different

Most food apps make you scroll through endless menus. Platter flips it: you tell it how you feel, it reads the weather, and it serves a short curated list of dishes that actually fit your mood.

And when you don't want to type — just tap the mic and say "Hello, show me happy food" or "Hello, go to cart". The app listens, understands, and acts.

🎙️

Voice is the primary interface

Voice commands use a wake phrase "Hello" followed by the intent. Quick-chip suggestions make discovery frictionless for first-time users.
